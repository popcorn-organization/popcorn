Privacy

Popcorn has no accounts, no ads and no servers of its own. Here's what it keeps and where.

On your device

My List, what you've watched and how far you got, your language choices, your settings and your Gemini key are kept on the device you use Popcorn on. They aren't sent to us. We don't run any server that could receive them.

Popcorn has no analytics, no tracking and no ads.

Crash reports

When Popcorn crashes, freezes or hits an error it can't handle, it sends a short report to Sentry, a crash-reporting service, so the problem can be found and fixed. A report has the error and where in Popcorn it happened, the app version, your device's model and system, and Popcorn's last few log lines, which can name the film or episode that was open.

Before a report is sent, Popcorn takes out links, keys, sign-in tokens and email addresses. Reports don't include your Google account, your Gemini key, your lists or screenshots, and Sentry doesn't keep your internet address (it notes only the country and town it comes from). Reports are stored in the EU and used only to fix Popcorn.

Syncing with Google Drive

Syncing is off unless you sign in with Google in Settings → Sync. When you do, Popcorn asks for one permission: to use its own hidden folder in your Google Drive (Google calls it "app data"). That folder doesn't show in your Drive, and with this permission Popcorn can't see, open or change anything else in your Drive.

In that folder Popcorn keeps:

  • My List,
  • Continue Watching, and how far you got in each film and episode,
  • the audio and subtitle language you chose,
  • which copy of each film or episode you last played, so another device resumes at the same moment,
  • your Gemini key, if you've added one.

Each device you turn sync on keeps one file there, and the devices read each other's to stay the same. The app talks to Google directly: the data goes between your devices and your Google Drive, and never to us. Popcorn also asks Google for your account's email address, only to show you in Settings which account you're syncing with.

This data belongs to your Google account, not to the device: signing out takes it off the device, and signing in again brings it back. Popcorn uses it only to keep your lists the same on your devices. It isn't shared with anyone, sold, or used for ads.

Popcorn's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Signing out and deleting your data

  • Sign out on one device: Settings → Sync → Sign out. My List, Continue Watching, your language choices and your Gemini key are removed from that device, and stay in your Google Drive.
  • Delete everything Popcorn saved: Settings → Sync → Delete synced data. This removes it from your Google Drive and from every device signed in to it. Nobody is signed out. Or, in Google Drive on the web, open Settings → Manage apps, find Popcorn and choose Delete hidden app data.
  • Remove Popcorn's access to your Google account: myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Other services Popcorn uses

  • TMDB and JustWatch, and a service for IMDb ratings, for film and series details, posters, ratings and where to stream. Popcorn asks them about the titles you look at.
  • Google Gemini, only if you add your own Gemini key, for Ask and Sinhala subtitles. Your questions and the subtitle lines being translated go to Google with your key. On Google's free plan, Google may use them to improve its services.
  • To play something, Popcorn connects to the sources the video comes from. Like any website you visit, they can see your device's internet address.
