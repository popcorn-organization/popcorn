Getting started

Is Popcorn really free?

Yes. There's nothing to pay, no subscription and no ads.

Do I need an account?

No. Open Popcorn and start watching. My List and what you've watched are kept on your device.

To move them to another phone or PC, use Settings → Export favorites & history, then Import from a backup on the other one.

Which devices does it work on?

Android phones and tablets, Windows PCs, and Android TV, Google TV and Fire TV. It isn't available for iPhone or Mac yet.

All downloads

How do I install it on an Android phone?
  1. Download the Android file.
  2. Open it from your notifications or Downloads.
  3. If your phone asks, allow install unknown apps for your browser.
  4. Tap Install.
Windows says it protected my PC. What do I do?

Choose More info, then Run anyway. Windows shows this for apps it doesn't know well yet, which is any app that isn't from the Microsoft Store.

How do I put it on my TV?

On the TV, get the free Downloader app, allow it to install apps once, and type popcorn-organization.github.io/popcorn/tv into it.

The steps, and where to find each setting

How do updates work?

When a new version comes out, Popcorn tells you the next time you open it and updates from inside the app. On Windows it installs itself and opens again. On Android, the first time, it asks you to allow Popcorn to install updates.

Watching

Why can't I play a film that's just come out?

Popcorn waits until a film is out to watch at home. Until then its page shows Still in cinemas or the date it comes out.

Which quality should I pick?

The one marked Recommended balances picture and download size. Each choice shows its size, so on mobile data or a slow connection pick a smaller one. 4K is there when a 4K copy exists.

You can change quality during a film from the player's menu. It carries on from the same spot.

Can I watch without internet?

No. Popcorn needs an internet connection while you watch.

The sound is in the wrong language

For films and series not made in English, Popcorn plays the original soundtrack. To hear English instead, open the player's menu and choose English, shown where a dubbed version exists. Popcorn remembers your choice for the whole series and never changes the language by itself.

Does it support surround sound?

Yes. 5.1 and 7.1 soundtracks are passed on as they are, so Dolby Atmos, spatial audio or Windows Sonic can use them where your device has it. On ordinary speakers and headphones the full sound, bass included, is mixed down for you.

Subtitles

The subtitles are out of time

Popcorn lines subtitles up by itself: it listens for when people speak and moves the lines to match. You'll see Syncing… and then Subtitles synced.

To adjust them yourself, open the subtitle menu and change the delay. Popcorn remembers it for next time.

Can I get subtitles in my language?

Popcorn finds subtitles in any language that has been made for the film or episode. Choose one in the player's subtitle menu; it becomes your default.

How do Sinhala subtitles work?

Choose Sinhala in the subtitle menu. Popcorn translates the English subtitles as you watch, starting where you are, and keeps them for next time.

This needs a free Gemini key (see below).

Ask and the Gemini key

What is Ask?

Tell Ask what you're in the mood for, like a feel-good film for a rainy Sunday, and it shows posters to choose from. You can ask follow-up questions, ask for what's on Netflix in your country, and write in your own language.

It needs a free Gemini key.

What's a Gemini key, and how do I get one?

A free code from Google that lets Popcorn answer you in Ask and make Sinhala subtitles. It costs nothing and you only do it once.

  1. In Popcorn, open Settings → Gemini key and tap Get my key.
  2. Google's page opens. Sign in with your Google (Gmail) account if it asks.
  3. Tap Create API key. If it asks for a project, choose Create project, call it anything, and tap Create, then Create key.
  4. Copy the key (a long code starting with AIza), go back to Popcorn and paste it.

On a TV, Popcorn shows a QR code instead: scan it with your phone and paste the key there.

The key stays on your device. On Google's free plan there's a daily limit, and Google may use what's sent (your questions and subtitle lines) to improve its services.
