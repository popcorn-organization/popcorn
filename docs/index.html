The player

Gets out of the way

The controls fade once the film starts. Intros and credits have a skip button, and quality and subtitles are one tap away.

Surround sound
Films keep all their channels, so Dolby Atmos on your phone, earbuds or PC can place the sound around you.
Subtitles that keep time
Popcorn listens to the film and lines the subtitles up with what's being said.
Light on storage
It keeps the last few minutes you watched and what's coming up, and clears older downloads by itself.
Every title

Its own page

Poster, cast, trailer and every episode, season by season. Choose a quality from SD to Ultra HD, or let Popcorn recommend one that balances the picture against the download.

Continue Watching keeps your place in each film and episode, and the next episode is ready when one ends.

Installing

One minute, once

Popcorn comes from this page, not an app store, so your phone or PC asks you to confirm the first time. Updates install over it and keep your history.

Android

  1. Download Popcorn on your phone.
  2. Open the file. If asked, allow your browser or Files app to install unknown apps, then go back.
  3. Tap Install and open Popcorn from your home screen.
Get it for Android

Windows

  1. Download the installer and open it.
  2. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Follow the steps and open Popcorn from the Start menu or your desktop.
