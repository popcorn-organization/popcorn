Questions

Is Popcorn free?

Yes. There's no account, no subscription and no ads.

Why does my phone or PC warn me before installing?

Because Popcorn comes from this website rather than an app store. The warning appears for any app installed this way. Allow it once and it won't ask again for updates.

Will updating delete my history?

No. Installing a newer version keeps your favourites, history and where you stopped in each title.

Is there an iPhone or Mac version?

Not yet. Popcorn is available for Android phones and tablets, and for Windows 10 and 11.

Something takes a long time to start. Why?

Some older or rarer titles are only available in copies few people share, so they load slowly. Picking a different quality often helps.
